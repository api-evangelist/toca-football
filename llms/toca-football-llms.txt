# TOCA Football

> TOCA Football, Inc. is a technology-enabled soccer experience and entertainment company headquartered in Costa Mesa, California, built on the training methodology of former US international and Premier League player Eddie Lewis. It runs two businesses: TOCA Soccer, a network of indoor soccer training centers across the United States, Canada and Mexico (private training, classes, clinics, camps, youth and adult leagues, tournaments and pickup play), and TOCA Social, a soccer-themed dining and entertainment venue brand. TOCA is the official soccer training partner of Major League Soccer.

TOCA is a consumer sports and location-based-entertainment operator, not a software vendor. As of 2026-08-05 it publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no API documentation, no SDK, no webhooks and no machine-readable API contract**. Do not assume or invent an `api.tocafootball.com`, `docs.tocafootball.com` or `developer.tocafootball.com` endpoint — none of those hosts resolve (DNS resolution failed on all three). The customer portal at `my.tocafootball.com` is a client-rendered single-page app backed by Google Firebase; it is an authenticated end-user surface, not a documented API, and it answers HTTP 200 with its HTML shell for every path, including `/openapi.json` and `/.well-known/*`, so a 200 from that host is not evidence of a document.

Its software ships only as end-user products: the MyTOCA player app (session booking, payments, performance tracking, player cards, leaderboards) and the TOCA Remote app (phone control of the in-center ball-delivery machine), plus in-venue smart-target and ball-machine systems.
